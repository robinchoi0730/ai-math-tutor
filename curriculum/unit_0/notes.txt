Unit 0: Summer Assignment & Graphing Review

Topics:
- Simplifying Radicals
- Simplifying and Evaluating Expressions
- Linear Equations/Inqualities
- Linear/Absolute Value/Piecewise Graphing
 
Teacher Expectations:
- Answers should only contain positive exponents
- All radical expressions should be simplified and rationalized if possible.


Common Student Mistakes:
- Forgetting to create two versions for absolute value equations
- Forgetting to distribute powers to negative values in a parenthesis
- Forgetting to switch the inequality direction when multiplying/dividing by a negative number on both sides.
- Forgetting to draw dotted vs solid lines for inequality graphing
- Forgetting to add open circles(holes) for piece wise function graphs.

Notes:
- This unit starts at the beginning of school year which is around Aug 15 and the unit ends at around Aug 23.
- The summer assignment is an assignment for students to do over the summer prior to starting this class.
